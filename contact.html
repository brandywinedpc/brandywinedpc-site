One request

Please don't email us symptoms.

Ordinary email isn't a secure or private channel, and we'd be doing you a disservice by inviting medical details into one. Questions about how membership works, what's included, whether Morgan is taking new patients, how this fits with Medicare - all welcome, all fine.

Anything about your actual health is better held until you're a patient, when there's a proper way to have that conversation. And if something is urgent right now, please don't email at all - call 911 or go to an emergency room.

This is also why there's no message box on this page. A contact form invites people to type things that shouldn't be sitting in a form database, so we left it out on purpose.
