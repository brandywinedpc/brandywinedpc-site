Before you decide

Three things worth saying plainly

This is in addition to insurance, not instead of it.

Membership pays for Dr. Katz's care. Your insurance still handles hospitals, specialists, surgery, imaging, and prescriptions. Please don't drop coverage to afford membership — that math does not work, and she'll tell you so herself.

Adults 65+ pay the same as everyone else.

There's no age surcharge here. Being over 65 often means being labeled "complex" and getting less time, which is precisely backwards. The rate is $150 a month, same as any individual, and $200 for a couple.

If the price is the obstacle, tell her.

Morgan would rather have an awkward conversation about money than have you quietly conclude this practice isn't for people like you. She can't make it free and won't pretend the number is nothing — but there is often more room than people assume, and asking costs nothing.

The panel is capped. Get in early.

Joining the waitlist costs nothing and commits you to nothing. It just means Morgan knows you're out there when enrollment opens.
