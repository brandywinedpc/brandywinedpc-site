Services

Everything a family doctor should have time to do.

All of it is included in membership. There is no per-visit charge, no add-on tier, and no service that costs extra because you used it more than average.

01 — The foundation

Primary care, newborn through nineties

One doctor for the whole household means one person who actually knows the family history — because she's been treating the family.

  • Sick visits, same or next day
  • Annual physicals and preventive care
  • Chronic condition management — diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid, cholesterol
  • Well-child visits, immunization guidance, and sports physicals
  • Women's health, contraception, and perimenopause
  • Geriatric care and medication review
  • Lab work interpretation, with time to actually go through it

02 — Genuinely different

Behavioral health, not a referral

Most primary care hands anxiety and depression off to a six-week waitlist. Dr. Katz treats it herself, as part of your care, with the enormous advantage of already knowing you.

  • Anxiety and depression — evaluation, medication, and follow-through
  • Postpartum mood and the fourth trimester generally
  • Stress, burnout, and sleep
  • Medication management with real follow-up, not a script and a shrug
  • Coordination with a therapist when that's the right call

Some situations need specialized psychiatric care, and Dr. Katz will say so directly rather than manage something outside her scope.

03 — Without the runaround

Weight and metabolic health, including GLP-1s

The hardest part of GLP-1 medications under insurance is rarely the medicine — it's the prior authorization, the denial, the appeal, and the coverage that vanishes in January. Direct pay skips that entire apparatus.

  • GLP-1 medication management — semaglutide, tirzepatide, and others
  • No prior authorization battles, because there's no insurer to ask
  • Metabolic labs, monitoring, and dose adjustment as often as needed
  • Nutrition, muscle preservation, and what happens after the medication
  • Honest conversation about whether a GLP-1 is right for you at all

Membership covers Dr. Katz's management of this care. The cost of the medication itself is separate, and she'll be transparent with you about what to expect from a pharmacy.

04 — In the office, or not

Procedures and telehealth, whichever makes sense

Plenty of things that get referred out — and billed with a facility fee — can be handled in the office in twenty minutes by a doctor who isn't watching the clock.

  • Skin lesion removal, biopsies, and cryotherapy
  • Laceration repair and wound care
  • Joint and trigger point injections
  • Abscess drainage, ingrown nail care, ear lavage
  • EKGs and in-office point-of-care testing
  • Video and text visits, included and unlimited

What's not included

We'd rather you hear this from us.

Membership pays for Dr. Katz's care. It does not pay for hospital stays, emergency care, surgery, specialist visits, imaging, outside laboratory work, or your prescriptions. Keep your insurance for those — and if you're ever unsure which side of the line something falls on, ask her.
